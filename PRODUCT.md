## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, UX/UI design leads, product managers, frontend teams, and AI product teams at large technology companies.

They are evaluating Chakhriya Korada for internship and cooperative education opportunities. Their primary job is to assess the quality of her product thinking, design craft, technical fluency, collaboration, and learning potential through credible project evidence.

## Product Purpose

This English-language portfolio presents Chakhriya Korada as a **Product Designer & Creative Technologist** who combines UX/UI design, product thinking, responsive frontend implementation, and AI-assisted development.

The primary visitor action is to explore selected project case studies. Secondary actions are to understand Chakhriya's process and skills, view her résumé, learn about her background and activities, and contact her about internship or cooperative education opportunities.

Success means that a hiring-team visitor can quickly understand Chakhriya's positioning, identify relevant work, assess her individual contribution and reasoning, and confidently continue to a case study, résumé, or future contact channel.

## Positioning

Chakhriya connects product-design decisions to technical feasibility. Her work spans user-centred problem solving, interface and interaction design, responsive frontend implementation, AI product concepts, generative-AI workflows, and AI-assisted development.

The portfolio must demonstrate this combination through process and working evidence rather than presenting design and implementation as unrelated skills.

## Operating Context

Chakhriya Korada is a Computer Science student in the Faculty of Information Technology and Innovation at Bangkok University. She is based in Bangkok, Thailand (UTC+7) and is preparing for internship and cooperative education opportunities.

The portfolio is public, responsive, English-only for now, and designed to be easy to update as evidence is assembled.

English is the only active language. The content architecture should remain straightforward to extend with a complete, manually reviewed Thai experience later; the live site must not expose incomplete locale routes or controls.

Required routes include:

- `/`
- `/projects`
- `/projects/[slug]`

No language switcher or visible Thai UI is required while the site is English-only.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with evidence.** Let selected case studies demonstrate thinking, craft, contribution, and technical feasibility.
2. **Make individual contribution legible.** Clearly separate Chakhriya's role and decisions from team outcomes.
3. **Connect design to implementation.** Show how user needs, product choices, AI behaviour, and technical constraints influence one another.
4. **Prioritise honest specificity.** Use confirmed context and results; label placeholders and unresolved facts rather than filling gaps with claims.
5. **Keep localisation extensible.** If Thai returns, release it only as a complete, manually reviewed experience rather than a secondary or mechanically translated version.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio must be keyboard accessible, responsive, readable at common viewport sizes, and usable with assistive technologies.

English typography must remain comfortable to read. Layouts must support varying line lengths and wrapping behaviour without clipping, truncating essential information, or relying on fixed-height text containers. Contrast must remain strong, and thin display typography must not compromise legibility, particularly on mobile.
